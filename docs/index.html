Landing in wind, turbulence and past a boulder

NEAT (evolving neural networks) starts from a network that copied a hand-written pilot, then works through a curriculum: calm air → a steady push → gusts → turbulence → a boulder → everything at once. Goal: ≥ 98% landed between the flags on 1,000 unseen flights, or stop on Wednesday evening.

Curriculum

Each stage's world, reward, pass check and the outcome I expected, written down before the stage ran. A pass check is 100 fresh flights with no training help, confirmed on a second fresh 100.

Progress

Landed rate per generation. Dots: the champion on 100 fresh check flights. Line: the champion on its training + selection flights (optimistic). Shaded bands: curriculum stages.

Log newest first

Updated automatically every 15 minutes from the training runs. Numbers in the log are measured, not estimated; "fresh" means seeds never used in training or selection.
